# Recent Talks and Appearances

We've been sharing our research on Bitcoin's quantum computing challenges at several conferences in late 2025. Here's a roundup of our recent appearances.

## Clara Shikhelman at TabConf 2025 (October 13-16, Atlanta)

Clara presented "Bitcoin & Quantum Computing" at [TabConf 7](https://7.tabconf.com/) in Atlanta, covering the threat model, current UTXO vulnerability analysis, and the timeline for implementing quantum-resistant solutions.

**Video:** Coming soon - some videos for the event are yet to be released.

## Clara Shikhelman at CCE 2025 (December 10-11, New York)

Clara presented at the [Columbia Cryptoeconomics Workshop (CCE 2025)](https://columbiacryptoeconomics.org/) at Columbia University, discussing the quantum threat to Bitcoin's ECC-based signatures, the scope of vulnerable funds (~30% immediately at risk), migration pathways (CDR, Hourglass, QRAMP), and the philosophical "burn vs. steal" dilemma for coins that fail to migrate.

**Video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jqrPndv7nzQ&t=1578s)

[Download slides (PDF)](/assets/blog/2026-02/Clara%20Shikhelman%20-%20Bitcoin%20and%20Quantum%20Computing%20-%20CCE%2025.pdf)

## Anthony Milton at Busselton Bush Bash 2025 (October 31 - November 2, Busselton, Australia)

Anthony presented "Bitcoin & Quantum Computing" at the [Busselton Bush Bash](https://bitcoinbushbash.info/), covering the threat model, current UTXO vulnerability analysis, and the timeline for implementing quantum-resistant solutions.
